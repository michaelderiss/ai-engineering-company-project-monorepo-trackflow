# TrackFlow — Delivery SLA Policy

## Standard Delivery Timeframes

TrackFlow offers the following standard delivery SLAs for shipments within the continental service areas:

### United States (Los Angeles operations)

| Service Level | Delivery Window | Coverage Area |
|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| Standard      | 2–4 business days | Continental USA |
| Express       | 1–2 business days | Continental USA |
| Same-Day      | Same business day | Los Angeles metro only |

### Spain (Zaragoza operations)

| Service Level | Delivery Window | Coverage Area |
|---------------|----------------|---------------|
| Standard      | 2–5 business days | Península Ibérica |
| Express       | 1–2 business days | Península Ibérica and Baleares |
| Same-Day      | Same business day | Zaragoza metro only |

## High-Demand Period Exceptions

During declared high-demand periods (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas season — November 15th through January 10th), standard delivery SLAs DO NOT apply. During these dates:

- **Standard delivery** is not guaranteed. All shipments may experience delays of 3–7 additional business days.
- **Express delivery** is not guaranteed. Estimated at 2–4 business days minimum, with no guarantee provided.
- **Same-Day delivery** availability may be suspended. Check with operations for current status.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT**: A TrackFlow salesperson or account manager must NEVER promise a delivery SLA during declared high-demand dates. The answer must always reference the seasonal delay policy and state that delivery times may be extended.

## SLA Measurement

Delivery SLAs are measured from the moment the parcel is picked up by the carrier (handover time) to the moment of successful delivery confirmation. Weekends and public holidays are excluded from count.

## SLA Breach Compensation

If TrackFlow fails to meet the agreed delivery SLA (outside of high-demand periods), the affected client brand may be eligible for a shipping credit equal to the cost of the shipment. SLA breach claims must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the delivery attempt date.

## Carrier Responsibilities

TrackFlow's carrier partners are contractually obligated to meet the SLAs outlined above. Any delays attributable to the carrier (SLA breach) are documented as a carrier_issue incident and may result in rate renegotiation or supplier suspension per TrackFlow's supplier management policy.
