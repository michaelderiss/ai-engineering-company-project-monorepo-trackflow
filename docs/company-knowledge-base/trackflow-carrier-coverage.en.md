# TrackFlow — Carrier Coverage

## United States — Los Angeles Operations

### Primary Carriers

#### UPS Ground
- **Coverage Zone**: West Coast
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: $7.45 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Primary carrier for local deliveries in Los Angeles and surrounding areas. Best for California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, and Arizona.

#### FedEx Ground
- **Coverage Zone**: Continental USA
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: $7.90 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Provides nationwide coverage across all 48 contiguous states. Higher rate than regional alternatives but offers broader reach.

#### DHL Express USA
- **Coverage Zone**: Continental USA + International
- **Service Type**: Last-mile and international delivery
- **Rate**: $14.20 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Used for urgent shipments and exports to Europe. Dual-category carrier (both last-mile and international).

#### OnTrac
- **Coverage Zone**: West Coast
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: $6.10 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Regional carrier. Best rate in the Los Angeles area. Coverage limited to California, Oregon, Washington, Arizona, Nevada, Utah, Colorado, and Idaho.

#### Laser Ship
- **Coverage Zone**: East Coast
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: $5.80 per shipment
- **Status**: Suspended
- **Notes**: Suspended due to incident rate above 8% in Q3. Previously covered East Coast deliveries.

### Reverse Logistics (USA)

#### ReturnBear
- **Coverage Zone**: West Coast
- **Service Type**: Returns management
- **Rate**: $4.15 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Returns management for Los Angeles customers. Handles inspection and sorting for returned products.

## Spain — Zaragoza Operations

### Primary Carriers

#### MRW España
- **Coverage Zone**: Península Ibérica (Iberian Peninsula)
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: €4.90 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Primary carrier for deliveries in Spain. Volume-negotiated contract. Best coverage for mainland Spain deliveries.

#### SEUR
- **Coverage Zone**: Península Ibérica + Baleares (Iberian Peninsula + Balearic Islands)
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: €5.20 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Broader coverage than MRW — includes Balearic Islands. Competitive rate for the extended coverage area.

#### DHL Express España
- **Coverage Zone**: España + Internacional (Spain + International)
- **Service Type**: Last-mile and international delivery
- **Rate**: €12.80 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Urgent shipments and exports from Zaragoza. Dual-category carrier.

#### Nacex
- **Coverage Zone**: Aragón y zona norte (Aragón and northern zone)
- **Service Type**: Last-mile delivery
- **Rate**: €4.60 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Regional carrier with good coverage in Aragón. Best option for rural areas in the Aragón region. Low rate for the northern zone.

### Reverse Logistics (Spain)

#### Logística Inversa Iberia
- **Coverage Zone**: Spain
- **Service Type**: Returns management
- **Rate**: €6.30 per shipment
- **Status**: Active
- **Notes**: Returns management for the Zaragoza warehouse. Handles inspection, sorting, and processing of returned products.
