# TrackFlow — Storage Pricing

## Standard Storage Rates

TrackFlow charges client brands for warehouse storage based on the volume occupied by their products. Rates are calculated per pallet position per month.

### Los Angeles (LA) Warehouse — Pricing

| Storage Type | Rate per Pallet/Month | Notes |
|-------------|----------------------|-------|
| Standard pallet racking | $45.00 | Up to 1.2m × 1.0m × 1.5m pallet |
| Bulk / floor storage | $35.00 | For large-volume, low-rotation products |
| Climate-controlled | $65.00 | For cosmetics and temperature-sensitive electronics |
| High-value / secure storage | $80.00 | For high-value electronics and premium fashion |

### Zaragoza (ZGZ) Warehouse — Pricing

## Minimum Commitment

All storage agreements require a minimum commitment of **3 months** per pallet position. Early removal before the minimum term does not release the client from the 3-month obligation.

## Volume Discounts

### Tiered Volume Pricing

| Monthly Pallet Volume | Discount | Notes |
|----------------------|----------|-------|
| 50–199 pallets | 5% off standard rate | Auto-applied |
| 200–499 pallets | 10% off standard rate | Auto-applied |
| 500+ pallets | Negotiable | Requires approval |

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT**: No storage discount above 10% can be offered without mentioning it requires **Miguel Torres's (Commercial Director) approval**. A salesperson must never commit to a custom discount without this acknowledgment.

### Promotional Discounts

Promotional discounts may be offered to new client brands for the first 3 months of their contract. Standard promotional discount is **15% off storage rates for the first 3 months**.

## Calculation Method

Storage fees are calculated as follows:

**Monthly Storage Fee = (Number of pallet positions occupied) × (Rate per pallet per month) × (1 − Discount percentage)**

- Pallet positions are counted at the end of each business day and averaged over the month.
- Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears.
- Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt.

## Currency by Warehouse

- All LA Warehouse fees are charged in **USD**.
- All Zaragoza Warehouse fees are charged in **EUR**.
- Currency conversion for multi-warehouse clients is handled at the client brand's corporate rate; TrackFlow does not provide foreign exchange services.
