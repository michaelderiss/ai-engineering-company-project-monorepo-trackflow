# TrackFlow — Returns Policy

## Standard Return Window

TrackFlow offers a **30-day return window** for all eligible products managed on behalf of client brands. The return period begins on the date of successful delivery confirmation to the end customer.

## Condition Requirements

For a return to be accepted, the product must meet the following conditions:

- Product must be unused and in its original packaging
- All accessories, manuals, and tags must be included
- The product must not show signs of wear, damage, or tampering
- Hygiene seals on cosmetics must be intact

## Domestic Returns (Same Country)

Domestic returns within the same country of delivery follow an **automatic processing flow**:

1. Customer initiates return request through the client brand's portal
2. TrackFlow issues a return label within 24 hours
3. Customer drops off the parcel at the designated carrier collection point
4. Return is processed at the receiving warehouse within 3–5 business days of receipt
5. Inspection and quality check are completed within 48 hours of warehouse receipt
6. Client brand is notified of the return outcome

## International Returns

International returns **require manual handling** and are NOT processed automatically.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT**: A TrackFlow salesperson or account manager must NEVER describe international returns as "automatic". They must always be referred to as manual handling procedures.

International return process:

1. Customer contacts the client brand's customer service team
2. Client brand submits a return request to TrackFlow's returns coordination team
3. TrackFlow reviews the request and provides a return authorization and shipping instructions within 48 hours
4. Customer must ship the product back using the specified carrier at their own cost (unless otherwise arranged by the client brand)
5. Upon arrival at the TrackFlow warehouse, inspection and processing takes 5–7 business days

## Return Costs

| Return Type | Cost Responsibility | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| Domestic returns | TrackFlow / Client brand | Return shipping covered per client agreement |
| International returns | Customer (or as arranged) | Unless the client brand covers it as a goodwill gesture |
| Defective/wrong item | TrackFlow / Client brand | Full refund of shipping costs included |

## Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of **15% of the product value** may apply for returns that are not due to a TrackFlow or client brand error. Client brands may waive this fee at their discretion for loyalty or goodwill purposes.

## Excluded Items

The following items are NOT eligible for return:

- Products outside the 30-day return window
- Custom or personalized products
- Products with broken hygiene seals (cosmetics category)
- Items damaged due to customer misuse
- Final sale or clearance items as designated by the client brand
